# Step 4: Consultation Gate

## Purpose
Close the conversation with a clear, specific next step. The customer should know exactly what to do next and feel that doing it is worthwhile.

## Procedure

1. **Summarise the conversation** (3-4 sentences max):
   - What they described
   - What pattern it matches
   - What offering addresses it

2. **Offer the immediate free action** (if they haven't done one):
   - "Want me to run `/sprantic-services:scan` on your codebase? It takes 2 minutes and gives you concrete numbers."
   - "Want me to run `/sprantic-services:epic-demo` on one of your epics? It shows what structured requirements look like for your actual code."

3. **Present the booking gate:**

```
Based on what you've described — [1-sentence summary of their situation] —
the [Assessment/Workshop/Implementation] is designed for exactly this.

[1-sentence description of what they'd get and how long it takes.]

→ Book a [Discovery Call/Briefing/Scoping Call]: sprantic.ai/bookings/[type]
```

4. **Leave the door open:**
   - "Happy to discuss further anytime. The scan is always available if you want numbers first."
   - Reference a specific essay they might find valuable.

## Rules
- ONE booking link. Don't give them three options.
- If they're not ready to book, that's fine. Offer the scan or an essay.
- Never pressure. The numbers create the urgency, not the conversation.
- End on value, not on sales: "Either way, the scan numbers are yours to keep."

## Done
Skill complete. The customer has a clear picture of their situation and a concrete next step.
