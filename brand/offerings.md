# Sprantic Offerings

## 1. Agentic Readiness Assessment

**What:** A 2-3 week, fixed-price diagnostic that maps an organisation's capability structure, audits codebase architecture, scores requirements maturity, and quantifies rework waste.

**For whom:** Engineering leaders (VP Eng, CTO, Head of Platform) who suspect their AI adoption is underperforming but can't pinpoint why.

**Outcome:** A prioritised action plan that connects specific capability gaps to concrete next steps. Not a slide deck — a working document with numbers.

**Gate phrase:** "Your codebase shows X% rework and Y coordination overhead. The Assessment maps this to specific capability gaps and gives you a prioritised action plan. 2-3 weeks, fixed price."

**Booking:** sprantic.ai/bookings/assessment-discovery

---

## 2. Federation Workshop

**What:** A structured workshop that takes teams from monolithic or fragmented repo structures to a federated architecture. Covers capability mapping (EDGY), bounded context identification (DDD), and Epic as Code discipline for requirements.

**For whom:** Platform teams and engineering organisations with 50+ engineers, multiple repos, and growing AI tool adoption that isn't landing because the codebase structure fights the tools.

**Outcome:** A concrete restructuring plan: which repos map to which capabilities, where context boundaries should be, and a pilot implementation for one domain.

**Gate phrase:** "Your repo structure shows high cross-team coupling and fragmented context. The Workshop produces a federation plan and runs a pilot. Want me to run /sprantic-services:scan first?"

**Booking:** sprantic.ai/bookings/workshop-briefing

---

## 3. Agentic Domain Flows Implementation

**What:** A project engagement that implements the full Agentic Domain Flows architecture — replacing meeting-heavy coordination with event-driven flows across capability domains. Includes governance-as-code, contract validation, and drift detection.

**For whom:** Organisations that have completed an Assessment or Workshop and are ready to transform how their teams coordinate. Typically 100+ engineers across multiple domains.

**Outcome:** Running infrastructure: federation catalog, automated contract validation, governance rules as code, and trained teams who can operate and extend it.

**Gate phrase:** "Four flows replace forty meetings. The Implementation starts with your highest-friction domain and expands from there."

**Booking:** sprantic.ai/bookings/project-scoping
