# Step 3: Relate

## Purpose
Map the customer's specific pain to specific Sprantic offerings. Not "we can help" — "here's the specific product that addresses exactly what you described."

## Procedure

1. **Summarise the diagnosis** using their words + your pattern names:
   - "You're dealing with [their description], which is what we call [pattern name]. It typically shows up as [symptom they mentioned] and costs [business impact]."

2. **Connect to the right offering:**

| Primary Pain | Offering | Why This One |
|-------------|----------|-------------|
| Unknown rework rate, unclear AI readiness | **Assessment** | Need baseline numbers before anything else |
| Known problems, need restructuring plan | **Workshop** | Have the diagnosis, need the architecture |
| Ready to implement, need execution support | **Implementation** | Have the plan, need the build |
| Multiple overlapping pains | **Assessment** first | Start with numbers, then scope |

3. **Explain what the offering delivers** (from `brand/offerings.md`):
   - What they get (specific deliverables)
   - How long it takes
   - What it costs (fixed price, time-boxed)
   - What's different about Sprantic's approach

4. **Reference relevant published content:**
   - "The Agentic Enterprise essay series covers the theory behind this — especially [specific essay title]"
   - "The whitepaper on Agentic Domain Flows shows the architecture we'd be implementing"

## Rules
- Map to ONE primary offering. Don't pitch everything.
- If they need the Assessment, don't skip to the Workshop.
- Reference proof points: "We built this for our own codebase — PAD manages 6+ federated repos with automated contract validation."
